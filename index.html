About Our Atelier

Where Artistry Meets the Art of Female Tailoring

At RIDA'S STICHES, we believe that clothes are not just fabrics sewn together; they are a celebration of femininity, self-assurance, and individual style. Founded and run with an uncompromising passion for couture by Perida Magaji, our studio is dedicated strictly to all varieties of female sewing.

Located conveniently along Sarkin Power Street, Bayan Dutse, Narayi, Kaduna, our workshop blends traditional craftsmanship with contemporary runway trends. Whether you are walking down the aisle, turning heads at an Owambe occasion, dressing for executive meetings, or updating your everyday wardrobe, we tailor to enhance your exact silhouette.
